The Retreat

A quiet table at
the edge of the sea.

Carved into the limestone, Villa Riviera is a retreat of twenty-two rooms and one unhurried dining room — where the menu follows the tide, the light and the small harvests of the coast.

Mornings begin with sea-air rituals and cold-pressed oils; evenings open with aperitivo on the terrace as the sun drops behind the headland. Our kitchen works with day-boat fishermen, hill-side growers and a single cellar of low-intervention Mediterranean wines.
