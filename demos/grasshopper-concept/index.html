Origins & Ethos

Crafted by Sonali Sattar & Himanshu Dimri

Grasshopper was born out of a desire to create a dining experience completely removed from city clamor. Situated on an open farmstead along Bannerghatta Road, dining here is not merely a meal—it is a serene communion with nature and culinary discipline.

Every table is placed under lush greenery, spaced widely apart to ensure total confidentiality and tranquility. There are no menus placed in haste; each course unfolds harmoniously according to the evening's bespoke harvest.
