XI Movements

Obsidian Reserve occupies what was once the strongroom of a private bank on Rue de l’Obsidienne. The vault door remains. Beyond it: eleven tables, a single open hearth, and a cellar that runs three metres deeper than the street.

Chef Ayaka Mori cooks with binchotan, aged sake lees and the discipline of a Kyoto kaiseki kitchen — tempered by a very Parisian appetite for butter, Burgundy and long conversations. Nothing arrives under a cloche. Nothing is explained twice.

Service is choreographed for twenty-four. There is no à la carte, no second sitting and no clock on the table. The menu is written each morning, after the market and before the first fire is lit.
