The Experience

A twelve-course dialogue between sea, fire, and time.

Aura is not a restaurant in the ordinary sense. It is a counter suspended above the city, where the evening unfolds at the pace of the kitchen — unhurried, precise, and entirely yours.

“Restraint, at this altitude, is its own luxury.”

The Michelin Guide

01

The Counter

Twelve seats of pale hinoki, facing a silent kitchen. Each course is composed inches from your hands, in the order the morning's market allows.

02

The Cellar

Forty-eight rare sakes, some older than the tower itself. Poured by our sommelier in hand-blown glass, matched to the weight and temperature of each course.

03

The Salon

A private room for ten, wrapped in smoked oak and city light. Your own chef, your own tempo, and a door that closes on the world below.
